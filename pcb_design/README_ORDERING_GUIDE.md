# 🛠️ 1-Minute JLCPCB Ordering Guide (Custom Bracelet PCB)

Follow these exact steps to have 5 finished, ultra-tiny (20mm × 16mm) boards manufactured and assembled:

---

### Step 1: Open JLCPCB
1. Go to **[https://jlcpcb.com](https://jlcpcb.com)**
2. Click **"Order Now"**

---

### Step 2: Board Parameters
* **Dimensions:** `20` mm × `16` mm
* **Layers:** `2`
* **PCB Thickness:** `1.2` mm *(ultra-thin for wearable)*
* **PCB Color:** `Solder Mask Black` *(looks premium)*
* **Surface Finish:** `ENIG (Gold)` *(best conductivity for touch pad)*

---

### Step 3: Upload Assembly Files (SMT Assembly)
1. Turn ON the toggle: **"SMT Assembly"** (Assembles all components automatically).
2. Upload **`BOM_JLC.csv`** (Located in: `C:\Users\haleemmamdouh\Desktop\Bond_Touch_Prototype\pcb_design\BOM_JLC.csv`)
3. Upload **`CPL_JLC.csv`** (Located in: `C:\Users\haleemmamdouh\Desktop\Bond_Touch_Prototype\pcb_design\CPL_JLC.csv`)

---

### Step 4: Confirm & Ship
- JLCPCB will show a 3D preview of your board with every micro-chip soldered on it.
- Select **DHL Express / FedEx** for delivery to Egypt (arrives in 4-6 days).
- **Result:** You receive 5 finished boards. You only solder 2 wires to the battery and 2 wires to the motor!
